    The graph opens grouped: each grey box stands for a group of topics that merge closely in the stored hierarchy. Click one to see what is in it, double-click to open it in place, and drag the resolution slider for coarser or finer groups. Click a topic to see its papers, or an edge to see what links two topics. Type in the search box to filter the graph to the topics you pick and everything related to them.
