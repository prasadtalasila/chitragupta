## Reader

An undergraduate studying cyber-physical systems: comfortable with
algebra and timestamps, has never operated a plant, is studying rather
than doing.

## Covers

Staleness as a definition, budgets derived from decisions, worked
examples with numbers, and the three behaviours of a stale read.

## Does not cover

Synchronisation strategies (the previous chapter in the imagined
course), clock synchronisation, and any implementation.

## Glossary

- **Staleness** -- the age of a reading at the moment it is used.
- **Staleness budget** -- the largest staleness a given decision
  tolerates.
