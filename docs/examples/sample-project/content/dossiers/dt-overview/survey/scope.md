## Reader

An engineer joining a digital-twin project who has read none of the
corpus and needs the map -- what the five papers agree on, where they
disagree, and what nobody has measured.

## Covers

The model/shadow/twin vocabulary, state synchronisation strategies and
operator trust, anomaly detection over twin state streams, and the
standards/registry side of integration.

## Does not cover

Tooling and vendor specifics -- no paper in this corpus names a
product, so the survey stays at the level of strategies and disciplines.

## Glossary

- **Digital twin** -- a virtual representation with automatic data flow
  in both directions between asset and model.
- **Digital shadow** -- automatic flow from asset to model only.
- **Staleness** -- the age of a twin's newest synchronised value.
