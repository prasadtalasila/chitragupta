## Reader

PhD committee reading the background chapter of a thesis on digital twins in manufacturing; they are researchers entering the area and need the map, not the lesson.

## Covers

What a digital twin is; how the twin is kept synchronised with the physical asset; factory applications of digital twins; interoperability standards for digital twins.

## Does not cover

Machine-learning anomaly detection, except where a source ties it to a twin.

## Glossary

- Digital Twin (DT): a virtual representation of a physical entity or system that spans its lifecycle and is updated from real-time data
- IoT: Internet of Things -- network of physical objects embedded with sensors and connectivity
- CAD: Computer-Aided Design
- CAE: Computer-Aided Engineering
- PLC: Programmable Logic Controller
